# Study page editorial standard

Local review draft. The churches page establishes the standard; 46 additional study/resource pages now use the same structure. The five informational pages retain their product-information purpose.

Each study includes its passage with a named translation, surrounding context, observations supported by verse references, interpretation with reasoning, one possible practical response, a page-specific exercise, a reading to avoid, and a prayer/follow-up. Examples are illustrative, not claims that every Christian must choose the same application.

Quoted text is stored in scripts/seo/scripture-excerpts.json with its original provider URL. BSB uses the app’s helloao provider; WEB and KJV use the app’s bible-api provider. Builds use these verified local excerpts and do not require provider requests. Editorial changes must preserve quotation wording and attribution. The SEO validator checks rendered quotations and coverage.

Related pages sometimes use the same passage study, with a distinct exercise for their method or audience. Future consolidation should be an explicit decision with redirects, not an incidental result of rewriting content.

Review priorities: confirm the interpretation against the passage and context; distinguish text from inference; check that the exercise fits the page’s promise; make sure application is concrete without being coercive; preserve material uncertainty.
